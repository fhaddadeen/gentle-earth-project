Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Gentle Earth Project ("GEP", "we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through this website, how we use it, and your rights under applicable data protection law — including the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Our company is registered in the Dubai International Financial Centre (DIFC), Dubai, UAE. Where we process personal data of individuals in the European Economic Area (EEA) or United Kingdom, we comply with GDPR.

1. What information we collect

a) Contact and registration form submissions

When you submit an enquiry or register for portal access via our website forms, we collect the information you provide, which may include:

  • Name, email address, phone number
  • Organisation name and job title
  • The message or enquiry you submit
  • Your selected stakeholder type and project interest

b) Geographic and technical data (form submissions only)

When you submit a form, we automatically collect your approximate geographic location by resolving your IP address through a third-party geolocation service (ipapi.co). This occurs only at the moment of form submission — not during general browsing of this website. The data collected includes:

  • IP address
  • Country, city, and region (approximate — not GPS-level precision)
  • Timezone
  • Internet service provider or organisation name

This information is used to understand the geographic context of inbound enquiries and to respond to you appropriately. We do not use it to track your browsing behaviour or build a profile about you.

c) Analytics data (cookies)

With your consent, we use Google Analytics (GA4) to collect anonymised data about how visitors use our website — including pages viewed, time spent, and general geographic region. This data is aggregated and does not identify you personally. You may decline analytics cookies via the banner shown on your first visit.

2. How we use your information

Purpose Lawful basis
Responding to your enquiry or registration request Legitimate interests / Pre-contractual steps
Understanding the geographic origin of form submissions Legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR)
Website analytics and performance monitoring Consent (cookie banner)
Fraud prevention and security Legitimate interests

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use it for automated decision-making or profiling.

3. Third-party processors

We use the following third-party services that may process personal data on our behalf:

  • Formspree (formspree.io) — receives and stores form submissions. Privacy Policy
  • ipapi.co — resolves approximate location from IP address at the time of form submission. Privacy Policy
  • Google Analytics (analytics.google.com) — website analytics, subject to your consent. Privacy Policy
  • Google Fonts — font files loaded from Google servers. Privacy Policy

4. Data retention

Form submission data held by Formspree is retained in accordance with their data retention policy. We do not retain raw form submission data on our own servers. If you contact us by email following a form submission, we will retain that correspondence for as long as necessary to manage our business relationship with you.

5. International transfers

Our third-party processors (Formspree, ipapi.co, Google) may process data outside the EEA. Where this occurs, transfers are made subject to appropriate safeguards, including Standard Contractual Clauses or adequacy decisions recognised under GDPR.

6. Your rights

If you are located in the EEA or UK, you have the following rights under GDPR:

  • Access — request a copy of personal data we hold about you
  • Rectification — request correction of inaccurate data
  • Erasure — request deletion of your personal data
  • Restriction — request that we limit processing in certain circumstances
  • Objection — object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Portability — receive your data in a machine-readable format

To exercise any of these rights, email us at contact@gentleearthproject.com. We will respond within 30 days.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

7. Cookies

We use the following cookies:

  • gep_cookie_consent — stores your cookie preference (accept/decline). This is a functional cookie required to remember your choice; it does not track you.
  • Google Analytics cookies (_ga, _ga_*) — analytics cookies, only set if you accept via the consent banner.

You can withdraw cookie consent at any time by clearing your browser's local storage for this site.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes. Continued use of the website after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
